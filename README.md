# Alem Robotics Showcase

Create a professional, modern website for ALEM Robotics, a student robotics team competing in FIRST LEGO League Challenge.

Team name: ALEM Robotics

Team number: 311436

DESIGN:

- Deep royal blue (#082B83) and navy blue background

- White typography with subtle cyan accents

- Minimalist, premium, professional technology aesthetic

- Subtle circuit-board patterns and smooth animations

- Modern typography, clean spacing, rounded cards

- Fully responsive for phones, tablets and computers

- All website content should initially be in Kazakh

WEBSITE SECTIONS:

1. HERO: ALEM ROBOTICS, subtitle "Болашақ инженерлері. Жаңа идеялар. Үлкен мүмкіндіктер.", TEAM NUMBER: 311436, buttons "Біздің команда" and "Жобаларымыз".

2. ABOUT US: Introduce the team and its interests in engineering, programming, innovation and teamwork. Do not invent achievements.

3. OUR ROBOT: Robot, missions, mechanisms, programming and engineering, with real-photo placeholders.

4. INNOVATION PROJECT: FIRST LEGO League season project, problem, research, solution, prototype and results. Do not invent results.

5. OUR TEAM: Editable member names and photo placeholders.

6. ACHIEVEMENTS: Editable timeline placeholders.

7. MEDIA: Training photos and competition videos.

8. SOCIAL MEDIA: Instagram and TikTok buttons with editable URL placeholders.

9. CONTACT: Contact section without publishing personal information.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:

Create a working website, not a mockup. Add navigation, smooth scrolling, accessible contrast, responsive layouts, tasteful animations and reusable editable components. Use image placeholders instead of fake team photos. Do not fabricate facts, team members, sponsors or awards. Make the homepage visually impressive and easy to navigate.
